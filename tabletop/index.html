🎨 Terrain look:
Plains, forest, rocky & sand each have 3 painted looks; mountains have 2 — 1 rocky, 2 ❄️ snow-capped peaks. Auto keeps one coherent look per map (mountains stay rocky under Auto; 🌲 Winter snow-caps them all); pick 1–3 to force one everywhere. Turn on 🖌 Repaint then click any tile to overwrite just that tile's look — with Auto selected each click cycles that tile through the looks, so click a mountain to toggle its snowcap and place peaks exactly where you want them.
