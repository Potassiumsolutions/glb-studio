Skeleton type

Pick the skeleton that matches your character's body plan — biped, quadruped, bird, snake, fish, cetacean, great ape, elephant, giraffe or dragon. Each maps to a standard rig, so exports are recognised by the Stitcher and its motion library retargets onto them.

2 · Fit the skeleton

ASnap to a view below (Front / Side / Top / Iso) or drag empty space to orbit freely. Right-drag pans; Shift+drag a window (or Shift+click joints) to select several joints and drag them together, wheel zooms. Then drag the joints onto your character — joints move in the plane you’re looking at.
