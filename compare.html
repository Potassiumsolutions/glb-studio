The short version

One toolkit for the whole job

Built for the whole job. Most tools do one slice: a site that animates a human clip, a desktop 3D suite, a model generator, a motion-capture app. GLB Studio takes you from a picture to a finished game in one place, in your browser. Make a model from a picture and it lands in your Studio Library with one click, rig it (people and 17 animal and creature types), animate it, film it, and put it on a print-ready board or into a playable app. It's built so you get results on day one, not after months of tutorials.

Made by a game creator, for game creators

Why I built this

I built GLB Studio because I needed it.

Making my pirate board game Port & Plunder and my card game Baby Dragon meant learning tool after tool: one for the card layouts, another for the box art, another to model and 3D-print the pieces, another to cut the how-to-play video, and a whole app-building setup to get the games onto Google Play. It took years of learning before I could make the thing I actually wanted to make.

GLB Studio puts that work in one place. Turn a picture into a 3D character, rig it and animate it, film your promo and how-to videos, build and print your boards, cards and boxes at Game Crafter sizes, print your pieces as 3D minis, and turn it into a playable app.

If it saves you some of the years it cost me, it's done its job.

Paul A.T. Ramey, KSOL Designs

Where GLB Studio shines

  • Picture to library in one click. Make a model with 🪄 Generate and it's saved straight into your Studio Library, already shrunk for games and ready to Rig or drop on the Tabletop. No downloading from one site and uploading to another.
  • The whole job in one place: model → rig → animate → film → board, cards, box → playable game.
  • Easy to pick up. Guided tabs and automatic fitting mean you're making things on day one, not after months of tutorials.
  • Animals and creatures, not just people: 17 types, from dogs and birds to dragons, crabs and manta rays.
  • Made for game creators. Boards and maps, cards, boxes and 3D-printable minis, print-ready for The Game Crafter, large one-piece maps up to 48×36 inches as print-shop posters, plus promo and how-to videos and an exported app.
  • It makes games, not just clips. Your rigged characters become playable pieces, and the game exports as a standalone app others can play.
  • Private & frictionless. The core tools run on your computer with no account and no install.
  • An original library you can actually reuse, free to use in your own work.

When something else may fit better

  • You only need a short animated human clip → Mixamo may be the simpler choice.
  • You're already fluent in Blender and want hand-keyed, frame-by-frame control → that's its home ground (new users should expect a steep learning curve).
  • You want to copy one specific real performance from a video → a video-mocap tool.

Try it yourself

Make a model from a picture or drop in your own, pick some motions, and export a video, a print-ready board or a playable game, free in your browser.
