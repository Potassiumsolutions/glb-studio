❔ Generate: how it works & what it costs✕

Making a model

  1. Picture: drop, paste (Ctrl+V) or choose a picture, then drag a box around the part you want. One object, plain background, whole thing in frame.
  2. What is it? 🏺 Prop for buildings, trees, vehicles, statues. 🧍 Character if you'll rig and animate it: use a front view in a T-pose (arms out). 📋 Copy a picture prompt gives you a ready-made prompt for Gemini or any picture AI.
  3. Quality: ⚡ Draft is fine for game pieces seen on a map. 💎 Hero is sharper, for close-ups, films and showpieces.
  4. Generate. The first model of a session can wait about a minute while fal.ai starts up; after that a Draft takes about 30 seconds.
  5. The model is shrunk to game size and saved to your ◆ Studio Library. Then use 🧍 Rig this (characters), 🎲 Use in Tabletop, or ⬇ Download .glb.

💲 Where the cost comes from

💲 Pay only for what you makeNo subscription or monthly fee. If you make nothing, you pay nothing.
  • The 3D model is made by TRELLIS, an image-to-3D AI by Microsoft, running on fal.ai's servers.
  • fal.ai charges your own fal.ai account for each model, using the key you set up in ⚙. You top up your balance on fal.ai; the money never goes through GLB Studio or KSOL Designs, and nothing is added on top.
  • You always see the price first: it's on the Generate button, each model shows what it cost, and 💲 Your costs keeps a running total and history. Prices are read live from fal.ai.
  • This is the only part of GLB Studio with a per-use cost. Rig, Animate, Stitch, the Tabletop and everything else run on your own computer and never charge per use. Add-ons and packs are one-time unlocks.
  • The AI Generate add-on itself is a separate one-time purchase that unlocks this tab. It doesn't include any fal.ai credit.

🔒 Privacy

Your picture is sent to fal.ai to make the model, and your key is sent only to fal.ai. Nothing goes to KSOL Designs. Everything else in GLB Studio stays on your computer.
